01 How do ZOLV strips work?

Peel open an individual sachet, place one thin strip on your tongue, and let it dissolve. Check the instructions for your chosen formula before use.

02 Are ZOLV strips safe?

Each formula contains different ingredients. Read the final label and any precautions carefully. If you take medication, have a medical condition, are pregnant or nursing, or are unsure whether an ingredient is suitable for you, ask a healthcare professional first.

03 Do ZOLV strips contain sugar?

The current ZOLV range is designed around a no-sugar concept. Ingredient and nutrition information must be confirmed on each final product label before purchase.

04 Can I take more than one strip per day?

Always follow the serving instructions for your specific formula. Do not exceed the recommended serving; some variants may contain active ingredients such as caffeine or melatonin.

05 Do you ship internationally?

Shipping destinations, delivery times and charges will be displayed once ordering is available. International availability has not yet been confirmed.

06 Which ZOLV formula should I choose?

Explore Energy, Sleep, Focus and NAD+ above to compare their intended routines and proposed ingredients. Use the final product labels to check the exact formulation and suitability.
